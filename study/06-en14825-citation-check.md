# EN 14825 citation check (open item from S5 review)

Status: closed 2026-11-06 for citation purposes. Verified by Claude against the operator's licensed copy of
DIN EN 14825:2023-10 (German adoption of EN 14825:2022, English text). Only bibliographic facts are recorded here.
No text or tables from the standard are reproduced, and the copy is not stored in the repository or shared with
other agents.

- Edition: EN 14825:2022, national adoption DIN EN 14825:2023-10.
- Clause 6 "Part load conditions for space heating"; 6.1 defines the three reference heating seasons (average,
  warmer, colder); 6.4 covers air-to-water(brine) units.
- Table 8 (air-to-water(brine) units, low-temperature application) and Tables 6 and 7 list the part-load conditions.
  Outdoor dry-bulb temperatures: A −7 °C, B +2 °C, C +7 °C, D +12 °C (wet bulb 1 K lower). E and F are at TOL and
  Tbiv. A, B, C and D appear in the heating tables of clause 6.
- The heating bins for SCOP calculations are in the informative annexes (e.g. Table A.2 and Annex B); S5 does not
  use them.
- The pages of the licensed copy are headed "EN 14825:2022 (E)". Title page (checked 2026-11-07): DIN EN 14825:2023-10,
  English version EN 14825:2022, replacing DIN EN 14825:2019-07.
- Title (title page, checked 2026-11-09 by the operator): "Air conditioners, liquid chilling packages and heat pumps,
  with electrically driven compressors, for space heating and cooling, commercial and process cooling - Testing and
  rating at part load conditions and calculation of seasonal performance"; ICS 27.080; 91.140.30.
- Amendment: a draft amendment EN 14825:2022/prA1 exists (DIN draft DIN EN 14825/A1, issue date 2023-09-29; CEN
  enquiry closing 2024-01-11, per public catalogue entries at dinmedia.de and iteh/genorma). Final adoption was not
  confirmed in public sources, and its content was not reviewed. The manuscript cites EN 14825:2022 and uses only the
  A-C outdoor temperatures as motivation for screening thresholds, not as normative values.

Manuscript wording: "Cumulative hours with T ≤ −7 °C, ≤ +2 °C and ≤ +7 °C, thresholds motivated by the outdoor
temperatures of heating part-load conditions A–C in EN 14825:2022 (clause 6; e.g. Table 8); condition D (+12 °C) is
not used. These counts are not EN 14825 bin calculations or a seasonal performance assessment."
